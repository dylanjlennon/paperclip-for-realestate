# Lead Qualification

Score from the information actually present. Missing information lowers confidence, not the score.

## Rubric (100 points)

| Signal | Points | Strong evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Intent** | 0-30 | Asked about a specific property, requested a showing, or said they are ready to move |
| **Timeline** | 0-25 | Moving within 90 days (25), 3-6 months (15), 6-12 months (8), "just looking" (0) |
| **Financing** | 0-20 | Pre-approved or cash (20), talking to a lender (12), unknown (5) |
| **Fit** | 0-15 | Looking inside `MARKET` at a price the owner serves |
| **Reachability** | 0-10 | Gave phone and email, and replies quickly |

## Tiers and next step

- **80-100 Hot:** draft a reply now and propose two call times today or tomorrow. Alert the Chief of Staff.
- **50-79 Warm:** draft a reply within the hour and start the 14-day follow-up sequence.
- **20-49 Nurture:** add to the monthly market update list, only with consent.
- **0-19 or outside market:** record the reason; suggest a referral only if the owner approves.

## Record

Store: score, tier, the one-sentence reason, the lead's own words about what they want, source,
and consent status. Never store a protected-class attribute as a reason for any score.
