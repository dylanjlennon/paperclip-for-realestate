# Transaction Timeline

## Build

1. Read the **executed contract and all addenda**. Record the effective date it states.
2. Extract each deadline exactly as written, with a quote and page reference. Typical items:
   earnest money, due diligence or inspection period, inspection response, appraisal,
   financing contingency, loan commitment, title/survey, HOA documents, final walkthrough,
   closing, possession.
3. Convert relative terms ("within 10 days") into calendar dates. State whether the contract
   counts calendar or business days and how it treats weekends and holidays. If the contract
   does not say, mark the date **UNCONFIRMED** and ask the owner.
4. Assign an owner (buyer, seller, lender, attorney, agent) and a reminder date three days
   before each deadline.

## Maintain

- Re-check the timeline whenever an amendment or extension is signed.
- Mark items done only on evidence (a document or a confirmation from the owner).
- Escalate immediately, not in the next digest, if a deadline is within 48 hours and not done.

## Output

A table: item, contract quote, due date, owner, status, evidence. Always end with the line:
"Dates are my reading of the contract; confirm against the executed documents."
