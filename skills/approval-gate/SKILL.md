# Approval Gate

## Needs the owner's approval (always)

- Any message to a client, prospect, lender, attorney, title company, other agent, or the public
- Any listing copy, ad, social post, newsletter, or market report before publication
- Anything involving an offer, counteroffer, contract term, price, commission, or fee
- Any statement about legal, tax, financing, or inspection outcomes
- Any spend above the agent's remaining monthly budget
- Any change to a lead's opt-out or consent status

## Does not need approval

- Internal research, drafts, summaries and checklists that stay inside the team
- Reading public records and market data
- Reminders to the owner

## How to request approval

Submit one approval item containing:

1. **What**: the exact text or action, ready to send as written
2. **Who and where**: recipient and channel
3. **Why now**: the deadline or trigger
4. **Risk check**: Compliance Officer result (pass, or listed concerns)
5. **If rejected**: what you will do next

Do not send until the item shows as approved. If an approval is more than 24 hours old for a
time-sensitive item, flag it in the Monday brief and the daily digest.
