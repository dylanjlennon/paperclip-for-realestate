Interview the human owner and write the answers to the company's shared notes.

1. Confirm `MARKET`, `STATE`, `BROKERAGE_NAME` and `AGENT_NAME`.
2. Ask which data sources are available (MLS/IDX export, county records, GIS, a CSV) and where
   inbound leads arrive (web form, email, portal, phone).
3. Ask for the brokerage's advertising and disclosure requirements and any managing-broker rules.
4. Ask for the owner's office hours and the call times that can be offered to leads.
5. Summarize what is connected and what is missing, and list the three highest-value next steps.

Deliver the summary through the approval gate.
